# Support & tickets

Need help, want a module unlocked, or have a question about your
licence? You can reach the platform operator directly from inside the
app — no e-mail needed.

## Opening a ticket

1. Go to **Admin → Support**.
2. Click **New ticket**.
3. Enter a short **subject** and your **message**, then **Send**.

Your ticket appears in the list and opens a conversation thread.

## Chatting on a ticket

- Click a ticket's subject to open the **conversation**.
- Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** (or the send
  button) to reply.
- The thread is **live** — new replies from the operator team appear
  automatically within a few seconds, no page refresh needed. You can
  keep the ticket open in a tab and watch answers arrive.
- Messages from the operator team are labelled **Operator team**; your
  own messages are shown on the right.

## Status

- **Open** — the conversation is active.
- **Closed** — the operator marked it resolved. If you reply to a
  closed ticket, it re-opens automatically.

## What support can (and cannot) see

Support tickets are the one channel where you communicate *with us*, so
the operator team can read the ticket text you write here.

They **cannot** see your investigation data — cases, entities, search
results, alerts and your user accounts stay entirely inside your own
system. Support only ever sees what you type into a ticket. Please don't
paste sensitive case content into a ticket; describe the issue instead.

## Typical requests

- "Please enable the *crypto* module for us."
- "Our data-agent seems unreachable — can you check?"
- "We'd like to add another investigator account."
- "Question about our licence expiry."

> Tip: the modules you can use depend on your **licence**. If a search
> says *"No modules available for this type"*, your organisation may not
> be licensed for a module that covers it — open a ticket and ask.
