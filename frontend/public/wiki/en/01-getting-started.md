# Getting started

## Signing in

1. Open the platform URL your organisation gave you. You'll see the
   **OOF** login card — subtitle *"Investigation platform for
   Swiss authorities"*.
2. Type your **work email address** (e.g. `firstname.lastname@authority.example`)
   and click **"Continue to sign in"**.
3. The platform looks up which organisation you belong to and redirects
   you to **your own organisation's login** for the actual password (and
   two-factor) step.

> **Privacy by design.** *"Your login credentials are processed
> exclusively by your own organization. The platform operator has no
> access to them."* The operator runs the software but never sees your
> password or your case data.

If you see *"Your organization is not registered on this platform"*,
your email domain isn't set up yet — contact your administrator. A
*"Connection error"* means the login service was briefly unreachable;
try again.

After login you land on the **Dashboard**.

## Finding your way around

A sidebar on the left has five sections:

| Item | What's there |
|------|--------------|
| **Dashboard** | Overview + a **"Start New Search"** button |
| **Cases** | All your investigations |
| **Search** | Start a search directly |
| **Monitoring** | Watch targets over time |
| **Admin** | Your session info (and, for admins, users/license/settings) |

The top-right header shows your **username**, a blue **tenant tag**
(your organisation — *"your data is scoped to this organization"*), a
**language selector** (EN / DE / FR / IT), and a **logout** button.

## The Dashboard

- **"Start New Search"** (top-right) jumps straight to the search form.
- Four stat cards: **"Active Cases"**, **"Running searches"**,
  **"Monitored Entities"** and **"Unread alerts"** — click the alerts card to
  open the alert list.
- **"What moved"** lists the cases with recent activity: new alerts, a search
  still running, or no activity for a week or more.
- **"Recent Cases"** lists your latest cases — click any row to open it.

## Choosing your language

Use the **language selector** in the header (DE / FR / IT / EN). Your
choice is remembered in this browser and also sets the language of generated
**PDF reports**.

Until you pick one yourself, the platform uses the **default language your
administrator set** for your organisation (*Admin → Settings → "Default
language"*); if none is set, it follows your **browser's language**.
